# social-media-guide skill

## Platform cheat sheet

| Platform | Char limit | Tone | Best times (UTC) | Media |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Twitter/X** | 280 (4K for Blue) | Punchy, opinionated, thread-friendly | Tue–Thu 12:00–14:00 | 1-4 images, GIFs, short video |
| **LinkedIn** | 3,000 | Professional, story-driven, data-backed | Tue–Thu 08:00–10:00 | Single image or document carousel |
| **Bluesky** | 300 | Casual, community-first, less corporate | Similar to Twitter | Images, GIFs |
| **Threads** | 500 | Conversational, friendly, emoji-OK | Evenings 18:00–21:00 | Images, short video |

## Universal rules

1. **Lead with the hook** — the first line determines whether someone scrolls
   past. Use numbers, questions, or bold claims (when true).
2. **One idea per post** — on Twitter and Threads, split multi-point content
   into a thread. On LinkedIn, use bullet sub-headings.
3. **Hashtag discipline** — 2-3 tags max on Twitter/Threads; 3-5 on LinkedIn.
   Prefer niche tags (`#OpenClaw`, `#DevOps`) over generic ones (`#Tech`).
4. **CTA clarity** — every post should ask for *one* thing: star the repo,
   read the blog, try the release, reply with feedback.
5. **Accessibility** — describe images in the text when possible (alt-text is
   not always visible). Avoid color-dependent diagrams.

## Cross-posting strategy

- Never copy-paste the same text across platforms. Adapt:
  - LinkedIn → full context, backstory, metrics.
  - Twitter/X → distilled hook, one stat, one link.
  - Bluesky/Threads → informal, ask a question to spark replies.
- Stagger posts by 30-60 minutes so each platform's algorithm sees fresh
  engagement rather than simultaneous cross-posting.

## What to avoid

- Unverified claims or speculation about competitors.
- Posting secrets, tokens, or internal URLs (even in screenshots — redact
  first).
- Engagement bait ("agree?", "thoughts?") without substance.
- Over-posting: max 1 digest per day, max 3-4 non-digest posts per week.
